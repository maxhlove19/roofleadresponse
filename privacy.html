Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Roof Lead Response ("we") is a service run by Max Love in Dallas, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Name, business name, phone number, email address and anything you tell us when you call, text or email us, book an audit, or become a client. For clients, we also process the contact details and messages of people who contact your business, only to reply to them on your behalf.

How we use it

To reply to you, schedule calls and audits, provide the service you signed up for, send service-related texts and emails, and keep records.

Text messages

If you text us or give us your number, we may text you about your inquiry or our service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text-message opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Who we share it with

Only service providers we use to run the business, such as our phone, email, calendar and messaging software, and only as needed to provide the service. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. To see, correct or delete your information, call or text (972) 895-9153 or write to the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
