Services

Every roof system, done the right way

Tile is the specialty. Shingles, flat roofs and decks are covered too, and as a licensed builder-general contractor Latchford handles the structural work around the roof as well.

Concrete & clay tile

Standard and lightweight concrete and clay tile, installed and repaired by the crew that calls itself the tile roof experts.

Reroofing

Building codes, product weight, insulation, attic ventilation, energy efficiency and warranties, all handled before the old roof comes off.

Leak diagnosis & repair

Proper diagnosis is the key to a permanent repair. Every leak is personally diagnosed by the owner, Brian Latchford.

Flat roof systems

TPO, PVC and SBS modified membranes for low-slope and commercial roofs.

Inspections & certification

Roof inspections, certifications, troubleshooting and ongoing roof maintenance.

New construction

Custom homes and commercial buildings, including the most intricate roof designs.

Also: asphalt composition shingles, waterproof deck systems, patio covers and gazebos, and structural retrofit and design.
